# Impact

## Users
Smallholder farmers near JKUAT, campus farm managers, water officers, and students learning applied climate ML.

## Decisions enabled
- Delay spraying or harvest drying when 3-hour rain probability exceeds 70%.
- Irrigate a measured millimetre amount before 08:00 EAT when the FAO-56 water balance drops below −20 mm.
- Rest, hydrate, and avoid outdoor work when WBGT is High or Extreme.

## Expected change
Fewer wasted pesticide applications, less over-irrigation, and earlier heat rest breaks.

## Cost of not having the system
A missed 10 mm irrigation on a 1 ha maize plot in a dry spell is typically a yield-risk event; a spray washed off by unexpected rain is a direct input loss; undocumented heat stress is a safety risk for farm labour during 10:00–15:00 EAT.
